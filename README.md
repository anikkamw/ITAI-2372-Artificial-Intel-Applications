## 🌟 Featured Project: MangeVision AI Diagnostic Tool
- **File:** [`2372_Module_3_Anikka_Williams.ipynb`](./2372_Module_3_Anikka_Williams.ipynb)
- **Domain:** Healthcare AI / Medical Computer Vision
- **Overview:** MangeVision leverages deep learning and Vision Transformer (ViT) architectures (`timm` & PyTorch) to analyze dermatological lesion images in companion animals. It automatically differentiates between *Sarcoptic* mange (Scabies), *Demodectic* mange, and healthy tissue to drive clinical decision support and rapid triage.
